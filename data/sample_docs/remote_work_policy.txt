Remote Work Policy

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week, subject to manager approval.
Fully remote arrangements require VP-level sign-off and are reviewed every six months.

Equipment: the company provides a laptop, monitor, and a one-time $300 home office
stipend for remote employees. Additional equipment requests go through the IT
helpdesk ticketing system.

Core hours: all employees, remote or in-office, must be reachable between 11:00 AM
and 3:00 PM in their local time zone for meetings and collaboration.

Security: remote employees must use the company VPN when accessing internal systems
and must enable full-disk encryption on any device used for work.
