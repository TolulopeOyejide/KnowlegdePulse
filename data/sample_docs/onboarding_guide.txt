New Hire Onboarding Guide

Day one: new hires receive their laptop and accounts are provisioned by 9:00 AM.
IT will schedule a 30-minute setup call to configure email, VPN, and internal tools.

Week one: new hires complete mandatory compliance training covering data privacy,
information security, and code of conduct. Training must be finished within five
business days of the start date.

Benefits enrollment: health insurance, dental, and 401(k) enrollment must be
completed within 30 days of the hire date through the benefits portal. Missing
this window means waiting until the next open enrollment period.

Probation period: all new hires have a 90-day probation period, during which
managers conduct check-ins at 30, 60, and 90 days.
