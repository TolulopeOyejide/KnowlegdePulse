Travel and Expense Policy

All business travel over $500 requires pre-approval from a department head before
booking. Flights should be booked in economy class for trips under six hours;
business class is permitted for flights over six hours.

Meal reimbursement is capped at $75 per day for domestic travel and $100 per day
for international travel. Itemized receipts are required for any single expense
over $25.

Reimbursement requests must be submitted through the finance portal within 30 days
of the expense being incurred. Requests submitted after 30 days require additional
manager approval and may be denied.

Client entertainment expenses require the client's name, company, and business
purpose to be noted on the expense report.
